# Sample photo sources

The five built-in sample photographs are public-domain / CC0 works, sourced 2026-11-20.

Every photograph was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and re-encoded once into the browser-sized
JPEG that ships in `app/samples/` (RGB, long side capped at 1920 px, JPEG quality 85). No cropping,
retouching or colour work was applied: the app's grade presets do that at render time.
